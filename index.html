Economics Information Management is a website designed to collect, organize, and display

important economic and demographic information from countries around the world.

Our main goal of this project is to solve a common problem: economic information is often spread across many different

websites, databases, and reports. Some websites may also contain old information, making it difficult for students,

researchers, or anyone interested in economics to quickly find reliable and organized data.

This project will create one easy-to-use website where users can explore and compare

important economic statistics for countries across different continents.

This website is also inspired by World Economics and IMF, we also get most of our information for them and the World Bank.
